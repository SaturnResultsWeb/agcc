    Why Alderney

    A small island with a first-tier standard.

    Alderney sits in the Bailiwick of Guernsey, within sight of the coast of France. Its regulator is built to keep one promise: the integrity of every eGambling activity it licenses.

    1. The regulator never sells the jurisdiction

      New enquiries go to Alderney eGambling, owned by the States of Alderney and run independently. The Commission stays free to judge every applicant on suitability alone.

    2. Clear law, applied the same way

      The eGambling Regulations, 2009 set out how every application is investigated, assessed and decided. Two licence categories, one standard.

    3. Answerable in public

      Four Commissioners oversee the Commission and report to the States of Alderney every year. The register, notices and reports are published.
